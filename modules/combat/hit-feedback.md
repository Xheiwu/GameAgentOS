# Hit Feedback System

## Category

Combat / Player Feedback

## Purpose

Create satisfying feedback when an attack successfully hits an enemy.

## Trigger

Player attack connects with target.

## Components

- Attack animation
- Hit pause
- Camera shake
- Sound effect
- Particle effect
- Damage number
- Enemy reaction

## Parameters

### Hit Stop

Short time freeze after impact.

Recommended range:

0.05 - 0.15 seconds

### Enemy Reaction

Can include:

- Flash
- Knockback
- Stun
- Animation interruption

## Design Goal

The player should clearly feel:

"My attack connected and caused impact."

## Engine Notes

Implementation should be adapted per engine.
